# Website marks

- `ia2-logo.svg`: original IA² monogram created for this website. Navy `#102d45`, white and turquoise `#70dfcf`. All lettering is drawn as vector paths so it does not depend on installed fonts. Used in the navigation and as the favicon.
- `iac-logo.svg`: unmodified official IAC colour vector mark, downloaded from https://www.iac.es/sites/default/files/2025-10/LOGO_IAC_2023_COLOR.svg on 2026-09-16. Source: https://www.iac.es/en/institutional-branding . The IAC mark retains its original colours, white background and proportions. Its 80px display height exceeds the manual's 60px minimum; the IA² mark precedes it with a vertical separator and surrounding clear space.

Meetings use `defaultTime` and `defaultLocation` in `data/meetings.json`, with optional per-meeting overrides.
